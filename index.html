Which format?

  • SVG: websites, and Word or PowerPoint in Microsoft 365 (stays sharp)
  • PNG web: websites and apps, 2× for sharp screens
  • PNG hi-res: older Office, Google Docs and Slides
  • PDF print: printers, signage and merchandise

Clear space and minimum size

  • Keep a gap of X on all sides, where X is the height of the dot in the mark
  • Wide with descriptor: at least 200 px / 45 mm wide
  • Compact with descriptor: at least 110 px / 25 mm wide
  • Below that, use compact without descriptor, or the mark

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows
  • Recolour the mark (Future Blue, black or white only)
  • Put the colour logo on Future Blue, mid-grey or busy photos
  • Rearrange the parts or use both descriptors together

03Colour

Click a colour to copy its HEX code. On light backgrounds, set text in Logo Ink, Deep Future Blue or Text Gray; Future Blue and Industrial Gray are too pale to read as text there. CMYK values are starting points: ask your printer for a proof.
